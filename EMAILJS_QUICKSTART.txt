# Email.js Setup - Sabari's Portfolio

## Quick Setup Steps:

### 1️⃣ Email.js Account
- Sign up FREE: https://www.emailjs.com/
- Get your Public Key from Dashboard
- Copy and paste into `js/script.js` line 2

### 2️⃣ Gmail Service Setup
- In Email.js Dashboard → Email Services
- Click "Add Service"
- Select "Gmail"
- Authorize your Gmail account
- Copy the Service ID

### 3️⃣ Email Template
- In Email.js Dashboard → Email Templates
- Click "Create New Template"
- Use these settings:
  - **Template ID**: `template_sabari_contact`
  - **Receiver Email**: `{{to_email}}`
  - **Subject Line**: `New Contact from {{from_name}}`

Template Body:
```
Hello,

You have a new message from your portfolio:

Name: {{from_name}}
Email: {{from_email}}
Project: {{project_name}}

Message:
{{message}}

---
Best wishes,
Contact Form from sabarimani.com
```

### 4️⃣ Update Configuration
In `js/script.js` around line 2-3:
```javascript
emailjs.init('YOUR_PUBLIC_KEY_HERE'); // Replace with your public key
emailjs.send('YOUR_SERVICE_ID', 'template_sabari_contact', templateParams)
```

## ✅ Testing
1. Open index.html
2. Scroll to Contact section
3. Fill form and submit
4. Check email within 5 seconds
5. Check spam folder if needed

## 🚀 Deployment
After setup is working:
- Visit https://vercel.com
- Click "New Project"
- Upload your portfolio folder
- Deploy instantly (FREE!)
