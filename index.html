The Appointment & Inventory Management System is a healthcare platform that simplifies patient scheduling, medical report handling, and inventory tracking. Clients can book, reschedule, or cancel appointments, while admins manage schedules with automated reminders. Medical reports are secured with role‑based access, allowing clients to download their own records and admins to upload or edit them. The system also tracks medical accessories with low‑stock alerts, automatically flagging required items when a treatment is booked to prevent last‑minute shortages.
